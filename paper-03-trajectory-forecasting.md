# Interpretable Trajectory Forecasting Through Embodied Cognition, Social Reasoning, and Physics-Aware Scene Understanding

**Journal:** Image and Vision Computing
**Manuscript Number:** IMAVIS-D-26-04959
**Status:** Under Review

**Authors:**
Majid Khorramgah; Mojtaba Rahemi; Amir Hossein Ahmadi; Hassan Nosrati 

## Overview

Reliable trajectory prediction is essential for autonomous systems operating in dense and socially complex environments. This study proposes an interpretable framework for motion forecasting grounded in **embodied cognition** rather than purely black-box data-driven prediction.

The framework combines a **Scene Attention Mechanism** for identifying potential threats, **Social Impact Theory-based graph reasoning** for intent inference, and a physics-compliant **Social Force Model** for realistic motion generation.

This integration enables autonomous agents to reason about safety, efficiency, interpersonal influence, and socially situated behavior. The framework is particularly relevant to **Embodied AI, Human Motion Understanding, Robot Perception, and Human-Robot Interaction**.

The study also incorporates interpretable outputs such as risk-sensitive heatmaps and interaction graphs, providing insight into the factors underlying predicted trajectories. The work aims to advance trajectory forecasting toward more transparent, socially aware, and physically grounded autonomous systems.
