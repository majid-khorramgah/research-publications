# Digital Twin Framework for Real-Time Monitoring, Prediction, and Control of 4D-Printed Continuous Carbon Fiber-Reinforced Composite Actuators

**Journal:** ACS Applied Materials & Interfaces
**Manuscript ID:** am-2026-18421t
**Status:** Submitted

**Authors:**
Hamid Reza Moetamedzadeh; Ali Shams; Majid Khorramgah; Mohammad Evazzadeh; Shahriyar Zolfaghari; Hassan Nosrati

## Overview

This work presents a digital twin framework for real-time monitoring, prediction, and control of 4D-printed continuous carbon fiber-reinforced composite actuators. The study focuses on integrating physical actuation, sensing, computational modeling, and data-driven prediction to enable intelligent monitoring and control of advanced programmable composite systems.

The proposed framework is designed to connect the physical behavior of the printed actuator with its digital representation, providing a foundation for real-time state estimation, performance prediction, and adaptive control. This direction contributes to the development of intelligent soft and morphing structures and highlights the potential of digital twins for advanced additive manufacturing and smart composite systems.
