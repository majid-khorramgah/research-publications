## Overview

Spatial intelligence is a fundamental capability for embodied agents operating in complex, dynamic, and physically grounded environments. This work introduces a brain-inspired perspective on artificial spatial reasoning, emphasizing how principles from biological cognition can inform the design of Embodied AI systems.

The proposed direction integrates **spatial learning, scene understanding, robot perception, and adaptive decision-making** into a unified framework for embodied agents. Such systems must interpret surrounding objects, infer navigable structures, maintain spatial memory, and coordinate perception with action.

By drawing inspiration from neural mechanisms of spatial cognition, the framework aims to support more robust learning under partial observability, uncertainty, and changing physical constraints. It also creates opportunities for combining **3D Vision, Reinforcement Learning, Imitation Learning, and Physics Simulation** in future embodied systems.
