# Behavior-Aware 3D Human Kinematics Estimation Using Activity-Guided Representation Learning

**Journal:** Neural Networks
**Manuscript Number:** NEUNET-D-26-08538
**Status:** Under Review

**Authors:**
Majid Khorramgah; Amir Hossein Ahmadi; Benyamin Safizadeh; Hassan Nosrati

## Overview

Accurate estimation of three-dimensional human kinematics is a key challenge in movement analysis, rehabilitation, sports science, and human-centered artificial intelligence. This study presents a learning-based framework that improves 3D human motion estimation by incorporating behavioral constraints derived from activity classification.

Rather than treating pose estimation as an isolated geometric prediction task, the proposed approach connects body movement reconstruction with semantic understanding of human activity. Activity-level context can therefore be used as a constraint on plausible joint configurations and motion trajectories.

The approach combines **spatial, temporal, and behavioral information** to support more meaningful motion inference. By embedding activity-aware constraints into the estimation process, the framework aims to reduce physically implausible predictions and improve robustness across different human activities.

The work has potential applications in **Humanoid Intelligence, Robot Learning, Human-Robot Interaction, 3D Vision, and Human Motion Understanding**, where understanding both human body configuration and the behavior expressed through movement is important.
